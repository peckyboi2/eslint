## Queue, overflow and recovery

[GitHub's current queue contract](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/how-tos/write-workflows/choose-when-workflows-run/control-workflow-concurrency) permits one running plus at most 100 pending workflows with `queue: max`; excess runs are cancelled. Ordering is by waiting time and is not an issue-order guarantee. Cancelled-before-start/overflow work can lack custom artifacts; Actions cancellation records and issue history are the recovery inventory. Inspect them after bursts or stop, keep unresolved reports as human work, and separately approve any unchanged-original-snapshot recovery plan/allowance. There is no automatic recovery trigger or unbounded durable backlog. Do not exhaust real quota for load tests.
